New Kingdom!

Last month a, kingdom west of spawn, was officialy established as "The Crowned Commonwealth pf Greenfieldshire", and in the following days Enriko Kundziņš the First, was officialy crowned as the king and rightful ruler of his land, previously already being renowned as by his people. The ceremony was held at the Palace of Jade Ridge, the king's castle. The ceremony started with his majesty Enriko Kundziņš the First being crowned with a netherite crown that is decorated with a golden jewel. The ceremony concluded with a firework show in the colours of the kingdom's flag.

This event marked a turning point in the history of the server, as the first ever officialy distributed and founded kingdom.
